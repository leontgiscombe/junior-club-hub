📋 Rules — What's Different in 7v7
R1
ALL Dribble-Ins — Use Them as a Weapon!
Under FA rules for Under 10s, there are no throw-ins — you pick the ball up and dribble back onto the pitch from the touchline. You must dribble into play first before shooting or passing — you can't score directly from the dribble-in itself. But here's the clever bit: if the opposition aren't ready, take it quickly before they can organise. Dribble onto the pitch fast and immediately shoot or play the killer pass while they're still setting up!

📐 7v7 Teaching Shape — 2-3-1 Formation
The 2-3-1 — Know Your Zone
Moving from 5v5 to 7v7 is one of the biggest steps in junior football. Positional zones are key — a team that holds its shape will find space around opposition who are chasing the ball. These zones are starting guidelines for our 2-3-1 formation: 2 Defenders (DR, DL) · 3 Midfielders (RM, CM, LM) · 1 Striker (ST).

DR & DL
RM & LM

🛡️ Defensive Zone — DR & DL
DR & DL Stay Back, Cover & Protect
The defensive zone is a guideline for both defenders. Stay in your own half when the opposition have the ball and be ready to defend any attack. Work together — one defender always picks up the opposition centre forward while the other covers behind.
Covering: If the ball is on the DL side, DR moves central to cover — and vice versa. One presses, one covers.
When ball is at the other end: Both defenders push up to the halfway line to stay compact and support play.
If you have the ball: Carry past the halfway line if there is space — but if pressed, look for a pass!

⚡ Wingers Zone — RM & LM
RM & LM Own Your Side of the Pitch
Wingers have free range the full length of the pitch — but only down their own side. Use the near post of the goal as your boundary line. This keeps the pitch wide and forces the opposition to spread, creating space for the whole team.
When ball is on the other wing: Tuck narrower towards the middle — not all the way in, but closer to the centre so you’re ready to receive a switch of play or make a run in behind!
Defend too: When we lose the ball, track back down your side to take up a good defensive position.

⚽ Centre Midfielder — CM
CM No Fixed Zone — Go Everywhere
The CM has licence to go everywhere — defence, attack, and both wings. Their job is to find space to receive the ball and support all areas of the pitch.
Defending: Drop back to help the defenders when the opposition have the ball.
Attacking: Push forward to support the striker and create chances.
Wide: Help out the wingers when they need an extra option.

⚽ Striker Zone — ST
ST Lead the Attack!
The striker operates in the opposition half of the pitch. By staying high, you keep the opposition defenders pinned back and create a target for passes forward. Drop to the halfway line to link play — hold the ball up, bring the wingers in, and turn defence into attack!
Stay high: Even without the ball, your position forces defenders to think about you and creates space for teammates.
Allowance: If a defender carries the ball out, you may need to track back and apply pressure on them.
