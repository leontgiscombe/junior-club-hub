Subs

Changes save as you make them. The team’s name and the season come from the club’s settings (Coach Admin → Settings).

Categories

What you pick from when adding money in or out. Renaming one changes the entries that use it; removing one leaves past entries as they are.

Players Who’ve Left

Players taken out of the hub’s Members tab, or marked as left on their card. They aren’t charged, and their payment history is kept. Tap a name to open their card, or Delete to remove them for good.

Backup & Restore

Your team's data syncs to the club database (stored encrypted). Exporting a backup is still a good idea before big changes. Note: the exported file itself is not password-protected, so store and share it securely.

Monthly Reminders

Get a push notification on the 1st of each month reminding you to check this team's subs and chase anyone who hasn't paid. Turn it on for each device (phone) you want reminded.

On iPhone/iPad you must first add this app to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there, then turn on reminders.

Security & Sync

Each team's data is encrypted in your browser with the team password before it is saved to the club database, so only someone with the password can read it. Changes sync automatically across devices. You can change this team's password below — it updates for everyone on their next login. If the password is ever forgotten, a club admin can reset it in Coach Admin → Settings → Sign-In & Passwords with the team's recovery code.

Devices & Passkeys

Phones, computers and passkeys that open this team with a treasurer’s or club admin’s account, without the team password. Each has its own key, so the records stay encrypted. Approve a waiting device here (you must have this team open), or remove one that’s lost or no longer used.

About & Privacy

This tracker holds children's and parents' personal details. It is stored encrypted, and only your team's treasurer, the club's admins and anyone with the team password can open it. Keep your devices locked, only record what you need, and delete a player's record when they leave the team. Share backups securely.
